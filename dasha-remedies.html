Understanding Kaal Sarp

The 12 Classical Variations of Kaal Sarp Yoga

Kaal Sarp Yoga occurs when all 7 visible planets are hemmed between the shadowy nodes Rahu and Ketu. Each house axis creates a distinct karmic test:

1. Anant Kaal Sarp (1st/7th):

Personality struggle & marital delays.

2. Kulik Kaal Sarp (2nd/8th):

Financial fluctuations & ancestral strain.

3. Vasuki Kaal Sarp (3rd/9th):

Sibling friction & delayed fortune (Bhagya).

4. Shankhpal Kaal Sarp (4th/10th):

Domestic unrest & career turbulence.

5. Padma Kaal Sarp (5th/11th):

Education hurdles & progeny delays.

6. Mahapadma Kaal Sarp (6th/12th):

Hidden adversaries & sudden expenses.

7. Takshak Kaal Sarp (7th/1st):

Partnership tests & public life hurdles.

8. Karkotak Kaal Sarp (8th/2nd):

Sudden setbacks & vocal discord.

9. Shankhachur Kaal Sarp (9th/3rd):

Higher education hurdles & paternal friction.

10. Ghatak Kaal Sarp (10th/4th):

Professional challenges & administrative checks.

11. Vishdhar Kaal Sarp (11th/5th):

Unstable gains & speculative losses.

12. Sheshnag Kaal Sarp (12th/6th):

Legal litigations & sleep issues.
