What is a Janam Kundli?

In classical Vedic astrology (Parashari Shastra), a Janam Kundli (Birth Chart) represents a celestial photograph of the heavens at the precise minute and geographic coordinates of your birth. The positioning of the nine planets (Navagrahas) across the twelve houses (Bhavas) forms an intricate tapestry of karma, innate talents, physical vitality, matrimonial destiny, and professional milestones.

Unlike generalized modern sun-sign horoscopes, a genuine Janam Kundli takes into account your exact Lagna (Ascendant) degree, which shifts every few minutes. Under the guidance of Shastri Amit Kumar Sharma, every reading is personalized, deeply analytical, and free from sensational fear.

The 12 Houses (Bhavas) of Your Destiny

Every department of human existence falls under one of the 12 Bhavas. During your consultation, Shastri Ji conducts an exhaustive house-by-house analysis:

1st Bhava (Tanu / Lagna)

Self-identity, vitality, physical constitution, character, temperament, and lifelong life force.

2nd Bhava (Dhana / Kutumba)

Accumulated liquid wealth, family lineage, speech, vocal tone, food habits, and facial features.

3rd Bhava (Sahaja / Bhratru)

Younger siblings, courage, entrepreneurial initiatives, short journeys, communication, and manual skills.

4th Bhava (Sukha / Matru)

Mother, emotional peace of mind, real estate, domestic sanctuary, luxury vehicles, and formal education.

5th Bhava (Putra / Purva Punya)

Progeny, past-life merits, intellect, higher learning, speculative gains, mantra sadhana, and romance.

6th Bhava (Ari / Rina / Roga)

Obstacles, debts, enemies, litigation, daily job routine, competitive exams, and physiological immunity.

7th Bhava (Yuvati / Kalatra)

Spouse, marriage durability, business partnerships, trade, foreign travel, and public interactions.

8th Bhava (Randhra / Ayur)

Longevity, sudden transformations, inherited assets, occult wisdom, research, and unexpected turning points.

9th Bhava (Dharma / Bhagya)

Divine grace, luck, father, guru, spiritual pilgrimages, philosophical depth, and charitable inclination.

10th Bhava (Karma / Rajya)

Career, social reputation, leadership status, government recognition, power, and professional zenith.

11th Bhava (Labha / Aya)

Financial fulfillment, profit streams, elder siblings, influential social networks, and goal attainment.

12th Bhava (Vyaya / Moksha)

Expenditure, foreign settlement, spiritual liberation (Moksha), sleep quality, meditation, and isolation.
